At the frontiers of statistical science

T. Tony Cai is the Daniel H. Silberberg Professor of Statistics and Data Science at the Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania. He has received numerous honors, including the COPSS Presidents’ Award, and is a Fellow of the American Association for the Advancement of Science (AAAS) and the Institute of Mathematical Statistics (IMS). He served successively as president-elect, president, and past president of IMS (2023–2026) and the International Chinese Statistical Association (ICSA; 2016–2018), and was Vice Dean of the Wharton School from 2017 to 2020.
